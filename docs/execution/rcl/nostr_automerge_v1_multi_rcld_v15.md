## Operation-ownership rule

The final inventory is derived from reachable source and is not assigned a
family count in advance. Every target-derived read, comparison, mutation,
allocation, insertion, traversal, or publication repeated by target size must
have exactly one approved owner. A presence check may share a lookup owner only
when the contract defines and proves the pair as one indivisible operation.
Once-per-build source-count reads, expected-count comparisons, completion
comparisons, and publication are explicit sealed constant-time operations.

The public Rust and independent TypeScript implementations may have different
concrete operation applicability. Every active item-metered row must have
nonzero reachable evidence in the implementation where it applies.
